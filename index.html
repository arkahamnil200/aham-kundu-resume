About

I am a first-year BBA student at IIM Bangalore who is most interested in problems that do not come with a template. My interests sit where finance meets law and policy: distressed assets, corporate restructuring, corporate law and the economics that shape them.

I am working towards a career in high finance, with a longer-term interest in doctoral research on distressed-asset investing. To build that foundation alongside my degree, I am preparing for the CFA, FRM and CA Foundation examinations.
